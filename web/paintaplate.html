          Starting

              Help

              1. Open a plate: Open, then ComfyUI files, Upload, Scenes or Blank plate. When ComfyUI runs on this machine, ComfyUI files also takes the full path of a movie, a frame of a sequence or a folder of frames. A blank plate starts a fluid shot.
              2. Pick a tool, Paint, Clean or Light, describe the change at the top, then paint it on the first frame.
              3. Generate: the edit on the first frame, then its shot through the whole plate. The Render panel shows each step as it runs.
              4. Play the shot, compare it with the plate in the wipe, then Export.

              Every action is in the right-click menu, with its key. The Keys tab lists them all.

              Paint
              Something new where you paint, as your text prompt describes it, following the drawing's outline, pose and size.
              Clean
              Removes an object, and the clean plate comes back behind it. Whole object hands the stroke to SAM 3.1, which finds the whole object under it.
              Light
              Light in the colour you pick, laid where it comes from; a dark colour lays shade. The eraser takes light away as softly as the brush lays it, so new light fills an erased place with no edge.
              Eraser
              Rubs out strokes of the edit in progress, or of the edit selected in History. A pen's eraser end rubs out too.
              Pipette
              On the first frame, the brush takes the frame's own colour where you click, so a stroke of it shows as the picture does.
              History
              One row per edit. Click a row to change its edit: its text prompt comes back; retype it, paint more or rub strokes out, then Generate. While it changes, the first frame shows the edits under it. Another tool starts a new edit.
              Undo
              One step per stroke, per removal, per change of text prompt or seed.
              Fluid shot
              On a blank plate: flat colour blobs on keyframes, such as light grey smoke, white steam, orange and yellow fire, and the shot described at the top. Keyframes snap to the first frame, the last frame and the 8-frame grid; drag a diamond on the timeline to move one.
              Close the plate
              × after the plate's name, or right-click > File > Close plate: every render stops, and its frames leave the browser's memory.
              Touch screen
              Two fingers pan and zoom the frame. ☰ at the top holds every action.
              On show
              Once the shot of the edits exists, it shows on every frame; the plate shows in the wipe and while O is held. A finished Render step, clicked, shows its pass alone; a second click, or Esc, brings back the latest.
              Wipe
              The plate on the left and the pass on show on the right, both named. A click on a Render step or a History row changes the right side. Ctrl+click on another step puts it on the left; Ctrl+click on the pass on show brings the plate back.
              X-ray
              Tints where the edits on show change the plate: the matte the composite of the size on show uses, or the export's once made. Before the composite, the tracking mattes. A Light edit tints the whole frame.
              Full screen
              The plate alone fills the screen, with no panel. The frame number shows in the bottom-right corner while the frame changes.
              Zoom
              As in RV, in plate pixels to screen pixels. Chrome keeps RV's Ctrl+2 to Ctrl+8 for its tabs, so Shift+2 to Shift+8 give 1:2 to 1:8.
              Cache memory
              View > Cache memory: the memory that keeps decoded frames, so the plate and the shot play smoothly. Auto sets it from this machine's memory; Custom… takes a size from 128 MB to 32 GB. A resize of the window keeps the frames.
              Playback statistics
              View > Playback statistics: play, then pause, and the log keeps a line on the playback.

              Colour (as in RV): display only

              Exposure, contrast, gamma, saturation, hue, the channels and invert change what the viewer shows, never the export. The values are RV's: contrast is 0 at rest and turns about 0.5, gamma acts before the other tools, invert before exposure. RV uses E, C, R, G, B and L without Shift; here P, C and L pick the tools, so the colour keys take Shift.

              The Colour Inspector reads the plate's own pixels and values, and the values on screen, on every frame. The brush still paints with a plain click. The page takes F5, so it does not reload.

              OCIO (as in RV): right-click > OCIO

              Plate (affects generation)
              Colour space: the plate colour space, what the file's numbers mean, by family (RV calls it File Color Space). The plate's colour space comes from its file: change it only for a camera's log footage. The line ends with "from file" when the value is the file's own, and "override" once you choose another. The models and the export use it, so a change regenerates the first frame and the edits. When an edit has a result, the page asks first. Display-referred: the family of the spaces encoded for a display, which RV and Nuke show as Display. The family ITU (International Telecommunication Union) is the one that RV and Nuke show as ITU.
              View (display only)
              View: the views of the display in use. Display: the other displays. Look: only when the config has looks. The viewer only, also while the shot plays.
              Config
              The config named by $OCIO when it is set, as in RV, else OCIO's built-in configs. It holds the plate colour space and the view, so a change regenerates the first frame and the edits. When an edit has a result, the page asks first.
              Steps
              Generate makes the edit on the first frame, then its shot through the plate. The Render panel shows each step with its time; a finished step, clicked, shows its pass alone.
              Preview
              Off: the full size only, with no live preview. Latent preview, the default: each pass shows live in the player. Half or quarter size: a first pass of the whole shot at that size, then the full size. The export stays at full size.
              Stop
              Esc, or Stop beside Generate: what finished is kept, and Enter goes on from there.
              Status line
              The latest event. An error shows in red and a warning in amber, until the next event that succeeds. The log keeps the details.
              Finish
              SDR to HDR regenerates the full-size shot in scene-linear values, fitted to the plate. Its result is half-float EXR frames in ACES2065-1, in the viewer and in Export.
              Export
              Writes the result in the plate's own format, with its matte, in ComfyUI's output folder, then downloads one zip, with the list of the models behind the result.
              Save scene
              Keeps the plate and every edit, with its strokes, text prompt and seed, on this ComfyUI. The log, the status line and the HUD give the scene file's full path; Open > Scenes opens it again. It asks before it replaces a scene of the same name.

              Edits

              P, C, L
              Paint, Clean, Light.
              N
              New edit: the next strokes start a separate edit.
              - and =
              Brush size.
              E
              Eraser: hold it while you drag, or tap it to switch the eraser on or off.
              I
              Pick a colour from the frame.
              S, T
              Colours, text prompt: in a dialog over the frame.
              Enter
              Generate.
              Ctrl+Enter
              Another seed for the selected edit, else the last one.
              Esc
              Cancel, deselect, end a comparison, then stop generating.
              Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z
              Undo, redo. Ctrl+Y redoes too.
              Hold O
              See the plate.

              Fluid shot

              Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V
              Copy the keyframe on this frame, paste it on another.
              Delete
              Delete the keyframe on this frame.
              Alt+Left, Alt+Right
              Previous, next keyframe.

              Playback (as in RV)

              Space, Down
              Play or pause.
              Left, Right
              Step one frame.
              Home, End
              Go to the in point, the out point.
              [ and ]
              Set the in point, the out point.
              \
              Clear the in and out points.
              Wheel
              Step through frames.
              F6
              Wipe between the plate and the pass on show.
              Ctrl+click a Render step
              Compare two passes in the wipe (Cmd+click on a Mac).
              , and .
              With the wipe on: the next pass on the left, on the right.
              X
              X-ray.
              `
              Full screen.

              Zoom and pan (as in RV)

              Alt + drag, middle drag
              Pan. Shift keeps to one axis.
              Ctrl + drag
              Zoom about the point pressed: right to zoom in, left to zoom out.
              f, Ctrl+F
              Fit the frame, fit its width.
              1 to 8, Shift+2 to Shift+8
              1:1 to 8:1, then 1:2 to 1:8, plate pixels to screen pixels.

              Colour (as in RV)

              Shift+E, K, Y
              Exposure, contrast, gamma: then drag on the frame, or use the wheel, - and =.
              Shift+S, H
              Saturation, hue, the same way. Backspace resets the one you edit; Esc leaves.
              Shift+C, R, G, B, L
              All channels, red, green, blue, luminance.
              Shift+I
              Invert.
              Shift+Home
              Reset all colour.
              F5
              Colour Inspector.

              Everywhere

              Ctrl+O, Ctrl+S
              Open a plate, save the scene.
              Right-click
              Every action, by menu, with its key.
              ?
              Show or hide the keys.

              Each model keeps its own licence: the one its Hugging Face repository states at the revision Paint a plate downloads. The app code is Apache 2.0.
