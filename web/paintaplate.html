          Starting

              Help

              1. Open a plate: Open, then ComfyUI files, Upload, Scenes or Blank plate. Sequence files and folders of frames are supported. A blank plate starts a fluid shot.
              2. Pick a tool (Paint, Clean or Light), describe the change in the text prompt, then paint it on the first frame.
              3. Generate: the edit on the first frame followed by the shot. The Render panel shows each step as it runs.
              4. Play the shot, compare it with the plate in the wipe, then Export.

              Every action is in the right-click menu, with its keyboard shortcut. The Keys tab lists them all.

              Paint
              Adds what the text prompt describes, with the outline, pose and size of your strokes.
              Clean
              Removes what you paint over and regenerates the background. With Whole object, SAM 3.1 finds the whole object under the stroke.
              Light
              Paint where the light comes from, in its colour. A dark colour paints shade.
              Eraser
              Erases strokes of the edit in progress, or of the selected edit.
              Pipette
              Click the frame to take its colour, then paint with it.
              History
              One row per edit. Click a row to see its strokes, or its pencil to change the edit. Another tool starts a new edit.
              Undo
              One step per stroke, per removal, per change of text prompt or seed.
              Fluid shot
              On a blank plate: paint your fluid (smoke, fire, water and more) on keyframes in flat colours, and describe it in the text prompt. Drag a keyframe's diamond on the timeline to move it.
              Close the plate
              × after the plate's name, or right-click > File > Close plate. It stops every render and frees the browser's memory.
              Touch screen
              Two fingers pan and zoom the frame. ☰ at the top lists every action.
              What the viewer displays
              The latest result of the edits turned on. Hold O to see the plate. Click a finished Render step to display it alone.
              Wipe
              The plate on the left, the displayed result on the right. Click a Render step or a History row to change the right side; Ctrl+click a step to put it on the left.
              X-ray
              Tints where the edits turned on change the plate. A Light edit tints the whole frame.
              Full screen
              The viewer fills the screen. The frame number shows in a corner while the frame changes.
              Cache memory
              View > Cache memory: memory for decoded frames, for smooth playback. Auto sets it from this machine's RAM.
              Playback statistics
              View > Playback statistics: play, then pause, for one line of figures in the log.

              Colour: display only

              Exposure, contrast, gamma, saturation, hue, the channels and invert change the display only. The export keeps the plate's colours.

              Use the Colour Inspector (F5) to read the plate's pixel values.

              OCIO (OpenColorIO): right-click > OCIO

              Plate (affects generation)
              The plate's colour space, read from its file. Change it only for a camera's log footage. A change regenerates the first frame and the edits; when an edit has a result, the page asks first.
              View (display only)
              The view, the display and the look (when the config has looks).
              Config
              The config that $OCIO names, or else OCIO's built-in configs. A change regenerates the first frame and the edits, as Plate does.
              Steps
              The Render panel lists each step of Generate with its time.
              Preview
              Stop
              Esc, or Stop beside Generate. Finished steps are kept; Enter continues from there.
              Status line
              The latest event: an error in red, a warning in amber. The log has the details.
              Finish
              SDR to HDR (standard to high dynamic range) regenerates the result as scene-linear, half-float EXR frames in ACES2065-1. Upscale makes Export also write a larger result, up to 3840 x 2160, with SeedVR2 7B.
              Export
              Writes the files you tick to ComfyUI's output folder, then downloads one zip. The result keeps the plate's format; with SDR to HDR, a movie or another SDR format gives half-float EXR frames.
              Save scene
              Saves the plate and every edit on this ComfyUI. Open > Scenes opens it again.

              Brush size.

              Another seed for the selected edit, or for the last edit.

              Zoom and pan

              Alt + drag, middle drag
              Pan. Shift keeps to one axis.
              Ctrl + drag
              Zoom around the point you press: drag right to zoom in, left to zoom out.
              f, Ctrl+F
              Fit the frame, fit its width.
              1 to 8, Shift+2 to Shift+8
              1:1 to 8:1, then 1:2 to 1:8, plate pixels to screen pixels.

              Colour

              Shift+E, K, Y
              Exposure, contrast, gamma: then drag on the frame, or use the wheel, - and =.
              Shift+S, H
              Saturation, hue, the same way. Backspace resets the value; Esc closes it.
              Shift+C, R, G, B, L
              All channels, red, green, blue, luminance.
              Shift+I
              Invert.
              Shift+Home
              Reset all colour.
              Shift+End
              Reset OCIO: the config's display and view.
              F5
              Colour Inspector.
