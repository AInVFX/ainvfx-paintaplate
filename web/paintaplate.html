Opening the plate

    Open a plate to start: a video file, or a folder of EXR, PNG, TIFF or DPX frames. You can also drop it here.

    History

      Your edits appear here. Describe, paint on the frame, then Generate. Click an edit to change its words or strokes.

      Fluid shot

      Paint smoke, steam or fire on the keyframes, describe it, then Generate.

      Render

        Click a finished step to show it on every frame; click it again, or Esc, for the latest.

        Deliver

        Passes that run after the full-size composite, each on or off.

        This plate's format cannot hold values above 1.0. With SDR to HDR, the result becomes an EXR sequence: the player shows its frames, and Export writes them as half-float EXR in ACES2065-1.

        This plate holds values above 1.0: its new content goes through SDR to HDR on its own.

        Starting

            Help

            1. Open a plate: Open, then ComfyUI files, Upload, Scenes or Blank plate. A blank plate starts a fluid shot.
            2. Pick a tool (Paint, Clean or Light: P, C, L), describe the change, then paint it on the first frame.
            3. Generate (Enter): the edit on the first frame, then its shot through the whole plate.
            4. Play it, compare it with the plate in the wipe (F6), then Export.

            Every action is in the right-click menu, with its key. ? shows the keys.

            Painting

            P, C, L
            Paint, Clean, Light. Clean removes an object, and the clean plate comes back behind it. Another tool starts a new edit
            N
            New edit: the next strokes start a separate edit
            - and =
            Brush size
            X
            Eraser: hold X while you drag to rub out the strokes you just painted. A tap on X switches the eraser on or off. A pen's eraser end rubs out too
            I
            Pick a colour: click the frame. On the first frame the brush takes the frame's own colour there, so a stroke of it shows as the picture does. Esc cancels.
            Enter
            Generate: the edit on the first frame, then its shot. Pressed again with nothing changed, nothing runs twice
            Ctrl+Enter
            Try another seed on the selected edit, else the last one
            Click an edit
            Select it: its words come back; retype them, paint more, rub strokes out or switch Whole object, then Generate. Esc deselects
            Esc
            Cancel a plate that is opening; else cancel the edit in progress, then deselect, then end a comparison in the wipe, then the latest result for a pass on show, then stop generating (Stop, beside Generate, does the same). What finished is kept, and Enter goes on from there
            × after the plate's name
            Close the plate (also right-click > File > Close plate): every render stops and its frames leave the browser's memory
            Two fingers
            On a touch screen: pan and zoom the frame. ☰ at the top holds every action
            Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z
            Undo, redo (Ctrl+Y redoes too): one step per stroke, per removal, per change of words or seed
            Hold O
            See the plate
            ?
            Show or hide these keys

            Shift+Home

            F5
            Color Inspector, as in RV: Shift+click a pixel to read it, Shift+drag to average; drag the box to move its point. It reads the plate's own pixels and values, and the values on screen, on every frame. The brush still paints with a plain click. The page takes F5, so it does not reload
            Right-click
            Every action, by menu, with its key

            RV uses E, C, R, G, B and L without Shift. Here P, C and L pick the tools. The values are RV's: contrast is 0 at rest and turns about 0.5, gamma acts before the other tools, invert before exposure.

            OCIO, as in RV: right-click, then OCIO

            The plate · changes the result
            Plate colour space: what the file's numbers mean, by family (RV calls it File Color Space). Its line says where the value comes from: read from the file, or chosen by you. The models and the export use it, so a change makes the first frame and the edits again. When an edit has a result, the page asks first
            The view · never changes a result
            View: the views of the display in use. Display: the other displays. Look: only when the config has looks. The viewer only, also while the shot plays
            Config
            The config named by $OCIO when it is set, as in RV, else OCIO's built-in configs. It holds both the plate's colour space and the view
